Write a handoff summary for continuing this work in a new session or with a different agent. Keep it compact - drop exploratory dead ends, keep decisions and evidence.

## Objective
What we're trying to accomplish.

## Evidence
Key findings, with file:line references. Not everything explored - only what mattered.

## Decisions
What was decided and why (not just what, why - so it isn't re-litigated).

## Constraints
Anything ruled out, any limits discovered (API limitations, permissions, deadlines).

## Relevant Files
Paths that matter for the next phase.

## Unresolved Questions
What's still open.
